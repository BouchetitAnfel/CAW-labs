Main Content

Since October 7, 2023, the Gaza Strip has witnessed an unprecedented humanitarian catastrophe. The ongoing military operations have resulted in devastating losses of life, widespread destruction of infrastructure, and a humanitarian crisis of immense proportions. The international community has expressed grave concerns about the scale of civilian casualties and the deteriorating humanitarian situation.

Casualties and Victims:

According to Palestinian health authorities, the conflict has resulted in tens of thousands of deaths, with a significant proportion being women and children. The Ministry of Health in Gaza has reported over 40,000 martyrs, including more than 16,000 children and 11,000 women. Additionally, over 90,000 individuals have been injured, many suffering from severe wounds that require urgent medical attention.

The humanitarian situation continues to deteriorate, with limited access to medical care, food, water, and essential services. Hospitals and medical facilities have been severely damaged or rendered non-functional, making it extremely difficult to provide adequate healthcare to the injured and sick.

The destruction of infrastructure has been catastrophic. According to estimates, over 70% of residential buildings have been damaged or destroyed, leaving hundreds of thousands of people homeless.



Critical infrastructure including water treatment plants, power stations, schools, and medical facilities have also been severely impacted, affecting the basic services necessary for survival. The impact on essential services has left millions without access to clean water, electricity, and healthcare facilities.

Infrastructure and Material Losses:

The material losses are estimated to be in the tens of billions of dollars. Key infrastructure losses include:

• Over 360,000 housing units destroyed or damaged
• Destruction of 200+ schools and educational facilities
• Damage to 35+ hospitals and 60+ primary healthcare centers
• Destruction of water and sanitation infrastructure serving millions
• Damage to roads, bridges, and essential transportation networks

The blockade and restrictions on humanitarian aid have led to severe food insecurity and the threat of famine. International organizations have warned that Gaza faces an imminent risk of widespread famine, with limited access to food, clean water, and medical supplies. Children are particularly vulnerable, facing acute malnutrition and disease.

The international community continues to call for an immediate ceasefire, humanitarian access, and accountability for violations of international humanitarian law. The situation demands urgent attention and action to prevent further loss of life and alleviate the suffering of the civilian population.
